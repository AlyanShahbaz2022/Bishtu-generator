# PRD – Tech & Tune E-commerce Platform

## Brand Name

**Tech & Tune**

---

# Project Vision

Tech & Tune is a premium industrial e-commerce platform specializing in diesel and petrol generators, generator rentals, maintenance services, spare parts, and complete power solutions.

The platform should allow customers to:

- Purchase generators online
- Request quotations
- Rent generators
- Book maintenance services
- Browse genuine spare parts
- Compare generator models
- Contact experts through WhatsApp, phone, or email

The website should combine the trust of a corporate industrial website with the convenience of a modern e-commerce platform.
