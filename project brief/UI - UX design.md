# UI/UX DESIGN BRIEF

# Tech & Tune

### Premium Industrial Power Solutions Platform

Version: 1.0

---

# Project Overview

Tech & Tune is a premium industrial power solutions company specializing in:

- Diesel Generator Sales
- Petrol Generator Sales
- Generator Rentals
- Repair & Maintenance
- Generator Overhauling
- Genuine Spare Parts
- Power Consultation

The website will function as both a premium corporate website and a modern e-commerce platform, allowing customers to browse, compare, purchase, rent, and request quotations for generators.

The design must communicate trust, engineering excellence, innovation, and reliability.

---

# Design Vision

The website should feel like a premium industrial brand rather than a traditional machinery supplier.

Every interaction should reinforce professionalism, quality, and technical expertise.

The design language should be modern, spacious, elegant, and conversion-focused.

# Success Criteria

The UI/UX should:

- Build trust within the first few seconds.
- Make navigation intuitive and effortless.
- Highlight products and services clearly.
- Encourage quote requests and online purchases.
- Deliver a premium, enterprise-grade visual identity.
- Provide a consistent experience across all devices.
- Balance aesthetics with usability and performance.
